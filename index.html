On the record

You are, in your own words, as quiet as a rock. Rocks are underrated. They don't flinch and they outlast almost everything in the room.

You deleted Instagram a year ago and never went back. You said you didn't want to get addicted to scrolling. Most people say that. You actually did it.

You read while you wait for your sister's chess class to finish. A whole hour that everyone else would have thrown away.

You won't start a good film until your head is clear — so you can catch every detail. Nobody watches things properly anymore. You still do.

Someone said something cruel about your friend's body and you didn't debate it. Your friends are lucky, and they probably don't say it enough.

You'd rather eat alone. Your reasoning: best seat in the house, and nothing gets shared. Difficult to argue with.

You write things down. Life, frustration, the odd interesting dream. Not for anyone else to read. That's the point of it.
